# Progression and content design

- Every new material should have a clear place in progression: where it is found, what it requires to
  obtain (tool tier), what it unlocks and why a player would want it over vanilla alternatives.
- Complete material set: raw item (raw_ore or gem), ore + deepslate ore, smelting and blasting recipes,
  ingot/gem, nugget (optional), storage block with reversible recipes, tools, armor, tags
  (e.g. c:ingots/ruby, minecraft:mineable/pickaxe, needs_iron_tool, beacon_base_blocks).
- Make content obtainable in survival: recipes, loot tables (chests, mob drops), trades or world generation.
- Recipes should cost resources consistent with vanilla (sword = 2 material + 1 stick, chestplate = 8).
- Unlock recipes with advancements/recipe book unlocking items so players can discover them.
- Add a custom creative tab for the mod and give it a representative icon.
- Interesting mechanics come from procedures: special abilities, set bonuses, custom interactions, but keep
  them understandable (tooltips via specialInformation).
